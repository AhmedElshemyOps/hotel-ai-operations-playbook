## What this project is

The **Hotel & Serviced Apartment AI Operations Playbook** is an open professional knowledge product for applying generative AI responsibly in real hotel work. It covers hotels, hotel apartments and serviced apartments across front office, reservations, housekeeping, engineering, guest experience, quality, SOPs, Lean Six Sigma, ESG, utilities, procurement, inventory, revenue, sales, distribution, finance, analytics, workforce management, general management and cross-functional control.

This is **not a collection of one-line prompts**. Every template defines the professional role, operational objective, property context, trusted inputs, output requirements, exception behaviour, evidence rules, privacy limits and accountable human approver.

## The HOTEL prompting framework

| Letter | Control | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **H** | Hospitality role & human owner | Define the professional perspective and accountable decision owner. |
| **O** | Operational objective & context | State the property, department, guest journey stage, timing and constraints. |
| **T** | Trusted inputs & sources of truth | Use approved PMS, RMS, CMMS, SOP, finance, HR, inventory and official sources. |
| **E** | Expected output, exceptions & evidence | Define format, evidence labels, missing-information behaviour and escalation. |
| **L** | Limits, privacy & leadership approval | Prevent invention, protect data and keep final authority with a qualified human. |

## Responsible-use baseline

AI output is a **draft**, not an operational confirmation. Room status, guest entitlements, rates, maintenance status, safety decisions, compensation authority, financial approvals and live availability must be verified through approved systems and qualified staff.

Do not place unnecessary guest identity data, passport or ID copies, payment-card data, credentials, confidential contracts, employee records or security procedures into unapproved AI tools.

## Development model

The repository is designed to grow article-by-article. Article 01 establishes the shared prompting and governance method. Articles 02–26 apply that method to specific hotel functions. Prompt IDs, datasets and navigation are reserved from the start so new chapters can be added without restructuring the product.
