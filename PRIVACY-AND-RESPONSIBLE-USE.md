# Privacy and Responsible Use

## Purpose
This policy defines the minimum responsible-use standard for the Hotel & Serviced Apartment AI Operations Playbook.

## Do not enter into unapproved/public AI tools
- passport, Emirates ID or identity-document copies;
- payment-card, bank or payment-token information;
- access codes, keys, credentials or security procedures;
- unnecessary guest names, phone numbers or email addresses;
- confidential corporate rates, contracts or supplier terms;
- employee records, disciplinary or sensitive HR data;
- CCTV footage or security-sensitive incident detail without approved handling;
- health or accessibility details beyond what is necessary and permitted for the task.

## Human accountability
AI output remains a draft until an accountable hotel professional verifies it against approved systems, current policies and real operating conditions.

## Operationally dynamic information
Always verify room status, availability, rates, restrictions, maintenance status, guest entitlements, staff rosters, supplier commitments, safety conditions and live financial data.

## Incident response
If an AI output contributes to a guest, operational, financial, safety, privacy or reputational problem: stop using the affected output; protect people and service; preserve prompt/input/output/review records; notify the accountable manager; assess data impact; record corrective and preventive action; revise the prompt or control before reuse.

## Legal note
This repository provides operational training material, not legal, privacy, cybersecurity, accounting or engineering advice. Organisations should use qualified professionals and applicable policies and law.
