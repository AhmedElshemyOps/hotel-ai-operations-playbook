# Hotel AI Authority Matrix

| Activity | AI role | Human review | Final authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draft guest message | Draft/support | Front-office or guest-relations employee | Employee within policy |
| Confirm room status | No independent confirmation | Verify in PMS + housekeeping | Authorized hotel employee |
| Recommend compensation options | Support | Duty manager | Role defined by compensation policy |
| Approve refund | No | Finance/operations review | Authorized manager |
| Suggest room rate | Analysis/support | Revenue manager | Revenue authority |
| Close safety incident | No | Qualified safety/engineering management | Authorized role only |
| Diagnose maintenance pattern | Analysis/support | Engineer verifies | Engineering authority |
| Change preventive-maintenance interval | Recommendation only | Engineering review | Chief engineer / approved authority |
| Draft SOP | Draft/support | Process owner + quality | Document approver |
| Approve supplier | Analysis/support | Procurement due diligence | Authorized procurement/management role |
| Change employee roster | Scenario support | Department head / HR | Authorized manager |
| Publish financial result | Draft analysis | Finance verifies | Finance leadership |
