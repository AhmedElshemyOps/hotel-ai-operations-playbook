# Prompt 244 — Guest-Risk-Revenue Integrated Review

**Article:** 25 — Hotel Control Tower & AI Decision-Support Toolkit
**Status:** Complete
**Department:** Cross-functional hotel management

## When to use
Use this prompt when hotel leaders need a structured guest-risk-revenue integrated review based on current operating evidence.

## Required inputs
- Approved source reports with timestamps
- Controlled metric definitions
- Current operating context
- Named process owner
- Documented decision responsibility

## Working method
1. Confirm the reporting period and source freshness.
2. Separate source facts from interpretation and recommendations.
3. Identify material exceptions and cross-department dependencies.
4. Explain assumptions and information gaps.
5. Name the accountable owner and expected completion date.
6. Define the evidence required to confirm completion.

## Expected output
Provide a concise management summary, source status, material exceptions, dependencies, options, actions, owners, dates and verification notes.

## Human review
Department owners confirm their operational information. The authorized hotel leader confirms material cross-functional decisions.

## Completion rule
When essential information or ownership is unclear, mark the item for verification rather than creating an unsupported conclusion.
