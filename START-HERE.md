## Before copying a prompt

1. Identify the accountable human owner.
2. Remove unnecessary personal or confidential data.
3. Replace every placeholder with confirmed information.
4. List approved systems and documents as sources of truth.
5. Tell the AI to expose missing, stale or contradictory information.
6. Separate facts, assumptions, recommendations and unresolved items.
7. Define what the AI cannot authorize.
8. Verify the output against the PMS/CMMS/RMS/SOP or other approved system.
9. Record human approval when the output enters an operating workflow.
10. Review the prompt when the process, technology, regulation or risk changes.
